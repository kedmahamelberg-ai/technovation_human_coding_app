# AI Posts Human-Coding App

A browser-based research application for systematically coding AI-related social-media posts.

## Contribution

The app guides coders through training, a codebook, six staged annotation sessions, attention checks and structured export. It is designed to make human interpretation visible and usable within a larger computational research workflow.

**AI as a research phenomenon:** the project studies how AI is represented through human outcomes—such as capability, opportunity, control, harm and system performance.

**AI as methodology:** high-structure human coding provides a transparent foundation for validating or complementing automated analysis.

**Teaching and impact:** a practical resource for teaching annotation design, research ethics, data quality and responsible AI evaluation. Its framing is relevant to organisations seeking to understand public narratives about AI.
