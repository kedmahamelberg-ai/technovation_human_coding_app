Quick codebook

Code only what the post explicitly represents. Do not judge whether it is true and do not give your own opinion. Human Experience and AI Role are separate decisions.

1. Human Experience

CodeChoose it when the post says…
EXTENSIONAI helps a person do an existing task better, faster, more easily, or at greater scale.
EXPANSIONAI gives a person a genuinely new ability, perspective, learning opportunity, or possibility.
RESTRICTIONAI limits or threatens human control, choice, privacy, or autonomy—for example refusal, surveillance, manipulation, dependence, or lock-in.
REDUCTIONAI is represented as actually diminishing human skills, livelihood, creativity, cognition, identity, or well-being.
NEUTRALNo clear human change is stated. Common examples: headlines, event announcements, product feature lists, and AI-art captions with no human effect.

2. AI Role

CodeChoose it when the post says…
AI_EXTENSIONAI works successfully, reliably, or usefully, or produces a useful completed output.
AI_EXPANSIONAI, its company, or its ecosystem explicitly gains learning, data, profit, adoption, market reach, or growth.
AI_RESTRICTIONAI constrains human agency: it blocks, refuses, censors, gatekeeps, manipulates, or induces dependence.
AI_REDUCTIONAI fails, hallucinates, deteriorates, produces poor output, or is represented as causing communal damage.
NEUTRALNo clear AI performance, gain, constraint, or failure is stated.

Fast boundary rules

  • Existing task improved → Human EXTENSION. Genuinely new capacity → Human EXPANSION.
  • “AI was tested” with no result → NEUTRAL, not AI_EXTENSION.
  • A product advertisement does not automatically create a Human benefit. Concrete successful AI functions may still be AI_EXTENSION.
  • “Smarter/better/faster output” is usually AI_EXTENSION, not AI_EXPANSION.
  • Bare AI-art hashtags or third-party credits are usually NEUTRAL/NEUTRAL. A creator explicitly showing their own completed output with a named AI tool may support EXTENSION/AI_EXTENSION.
  • Non-generative robotics or an unconnected #AI tag → NEUTRAL/NEUTRAL.
  • Possible dependence or manipulative attachment → RESTRICTION/AI_RESTRICTION. Realized loss of skill or well-being → REDUCTION.

Confidence

  • HIGH: the post states the signal directly.
  • MEDIUM: the label is supported but requires some interpretation.
  • LOW: genuinely ambiguous; you are making your best choice.
