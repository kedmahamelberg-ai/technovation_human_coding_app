Quick codebook

Code what the post states or clearly implies. Use only the words and meaning in the post. Do not decide whether it is true, add missing information, or give your own opinion.

For every post, answer two separate questions. A post can affect people and portray AI differently.

1. What happens to people?

Choose this explanationMeaning
AI helps people do something they already doThe task becomes better, faster, easier, or possible at a larger scale.
AI gives people a new ability or possibilityThe person gains a genuinely new capacity, perspective, learning opportunity, or option.
AI limits people’s control or choicesThe post states or clearly implies refusal, surveillance, manipulation, dependence, loss of privacy, or lock-in.
AI causes people to lose skills or well-beingThe post represents an actual reduction in skills, livelihood, creativity, thinking, identity, or well-being.
No clear effect on peopleNo human change is stated or clearly implied. Examples include headlines, event announcements, feature lists, or AI-art captions without a human effect.

2. What role does AI play?

Choose this explanationMeaning
AI works or produces a useful resultAI is represented as successful, reliable, useful, or producing a useful completed output.
AI or its company gains data, users, learning, or growthThe post states or clearly implies learning, data collection, profit, adoption, market reach, or ecosystem growth.
AI blocks, manipulates, or controls peopleAI refuses, censors, gatekeeps, manipulates, surveils, or creates dependence.
AI fails, performs badly, or causes broader harmAI hallucinates, deteriorates, produces poor output, fails, or is represented as causing communal damage.
No clear result for AINo clear AI success, gain, constraint, or failure is stated or clearly implied.

Fast boundary rules

  • An existing task becomes easier or better → choose “helps people do something they already do.”
  • A genuinely new human capacity appears → choose “gives people a new ability or possibility.”
  • “AI was tested” without a result → choose “No clear result for AI.”
  • A product advertisement does not automatically prove an effect on people. It may still clearly claim that AI performs useful functions.
  • “Smarter,” “better,” or “faster” output usually means that AI works; it does not by itself mean that AI or its company grows.
  • Bare AI-art hashtags, third-party credits, non-generative robotics, or an unrelated #AI tag usually provide no clear effect on people and no clear result for AI.
  • Possible dependence or manipulation concerns control and choice. An actual loss of skill or well-being concerns a realized human loss.
